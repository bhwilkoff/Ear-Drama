Security

Vulnerability disclosure policy. Last updated November 21, 2026.

Report a problem

If you find a way to see or change information you should not, in the website, the apps, or our server, write to ben@learningischange.com with what you found and how to see it again.

We will answer within 5 days, keep you told while we fix it, and thank you by name when it is fixed if you would like that. We pay no rewards.

What we ask

  • Test only with your own account, your own class, and your own recordings.
  • Never open, keep, change, or share another person's information. If you reach some by accident, stop, and tell us what you saw.
  • No denial of service, no spam, no social engineering, and no physical attacks.
  • Give us 90 days to fix a problem before you tell anyone else.

If you follow these, we will not take legal action against you for your research, and we will say so to anyone who asks.

In scope

  • eardrama.org and its pages
  • Our server's routes (sign-in, sync, rooms, plays, classes)
  • The Ear Drama apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, and Android

The Internet Archive, Cloudflare, GitHub, Apple, and Google run their own programs; report their problems to them.
