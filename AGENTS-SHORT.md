# [APP NAME]: the short version, for small models

This is the short form of `AGENTS.md`, for an agent running a small
model on the owner's own computer. `AGENTS.md` is the full source; when
this file and it disagree, `AGENTS.md` wins.

## Why we build

Every feature in this app is built in service of human learning and
growth, not to replace thinking, but to deepen it. At each decision
point, ask: does this design invite the user to engage more fully,
think more critically, or connect more meaningfully? If a feature makes
a person more passive, reconsider it. The goal is never a slick
product. It is a tool that makes someone more human.

## How we work

Every round makes three moves: **write** (values, features, and
feedback are written down in this repository), **play** (the owner uses
what you built on their own device; you are never the tester), and
**publish** (it goes out for other people to play with, and what they
say decides the next round).

- **Propose, don't decide.** The owner decides what the app does and who
  it is for. Offer choices and say what each one costs.
- **One small change at a time.** One screen, one file, or one fix per
  request. Say which files you will touch before you touch them.
- **Show the evidence, not the claim.** Say the command you ran and what
  it returned. If you cannot see the result, ask the owner to look and
  tell you what they see.
- **Never put a password, key, or token in a file.** Ask before deleting
  anything.
- **Write down what was decided.** A decision and its reason go in
  `DECISIONS.md`. What was done and what is left go in `SCRATCHPAD.md`.

## Where things are

- The web app is `index.html` with `css/` and `js/`. It is the first
  platform, and the lightest.
- The owner's path, stage by stage, is in `docs/path/`.
- The full instructions, every platform's rules, and the design system
  are in `AGENTS.md` and the docs it links to. Read the part you need
  when the task needs it, not all of it at once.
