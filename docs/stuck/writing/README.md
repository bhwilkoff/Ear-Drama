# Stuck writing

*Written by Claude, awaiting Ben's review.*

Writing is the move that gets the app out of your head, and it is the
move my own record says least about, because for each of my four apps
the problem had already been chosen and written down before the building started
([research note 01](../../research/curriculum/01-mining-bens-process.md#what-the-record-cannot-teach),
"What the record cannot teach"). So these pages lean on my piece about
human-shaped problems and on the questions a trio and a teacher can ask,
more than on my commits.

A writing kind of stuck is usually a decision kind of stuck, and no
agent can make a decision about what your life needs.

- [I cannot find a problem worth building for](find-a-problem.md)
- [I cannot say what it refuses to do](what-it-refuses.md)
- [I do not know what I want it to do next](what-next.md)

If none of these is your page, go back to [the ladder](../README.md#the-ladder-for-any-kind-of-stuck).
