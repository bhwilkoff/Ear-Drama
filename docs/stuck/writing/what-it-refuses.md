# I cannot say what it refuses to do

*Written by Claude, awaiting Ben's review.*

Every one of my apps has a sentence near the top that says what it will
not do, and that sentence ends up deciding more than any feature list.
Archive Watch's first decisions, logged the day after its first real
commit, include "No user accounts; all state local" (Archive-Watch
4162f80, April 18, 2026), and Tidbits Trivia's whole paywall was decided
by one line I wrote back to the agent: "the whole point of Tidbits
Trivia is that we are the world's best trivia app with the least amount
behind a paywall" (stage 00, [`00-why-we-build.md`](../../path/00-why-we-build.md)).
When you cannot write that sentence yet, the app has no edges, and the
agent will happily fill the space with whatever is common.

**Ask yourself first.** What would make me stop using this app, or feel
bad about having it on my phone?

**Try this.** Write your answer down as a rule in the first person ("It
never ..."), and then let the agent argue with it, which is a move from
[talking to your agent](../../path/talking-to-your-agent.md#ask-it-to-argue-the-other-side):

> Here is the rule I want this app to keep, and why. Argue the other
> side for a minute: what would an app that breaks this rule gain, and
> who would it cost? Then help me say the rule in one plain sentence.

Keep the version that still sounds like you, and put it in your app's
why, where your agent reads it at the start of every session.

**From my apps.** Bsky Dreams names what it optimizes for instead of
time on the platform: "does this invite deeper engagement, more critical
thinking, or more meaningful connection?" (Bsky-Dreams README, read in
[research note 01](../../research/curriculum/01-mining-bens-process.md#1-living-with-the-hum)).

**In any tool.** The rule goes in `AGENTS.md`, which Claude Code (through
`CLAUDE.md`), Antigravity, and the open path's agents all read. On the
open path with a small model, keep the rule near the top, because the
model may only hold the first part of a long file
([research note 04](../../research/curriculum/04-open-pathway.md)).

**Go deeper.** Hold your rule up against the fifteen
[principles](../../human-shaped/PRINCIPLES.md) and name the one it
protects.

**Who to ask.** One of the people your app is for, outside your screen,
because they will tell you what would make them stop faster than anyone
in the cohort can.

One rule, in your own words, at the top.
