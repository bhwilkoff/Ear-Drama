# I do not know when it is enough

*Written by Claude, awaiting Ben's review.*

There is always one more feature, and the agent will always build it, so
"done" never arrives on its own. My own record has no moment where I
decided a version of an app was finished for its people, only moments
where a store or a tester forced the question
([research note 01](../../research/curriculum/01-mining-bens-process.md#what-the-record-cannot-teach)),
which is why this page leans on the people you named rather than on my
commits.

**Ask yourself first.** Does it bring joy to at least one person I named
at the start, in the form it ships today? That is the fifteenth
principle, word for word: a human-shaped app "brings joy to at least one
person in the form it ships today" ([PRINCIPLES.md](../../human-shaped/PRINCIPLES.md)).

**Try this.** Go back to the people you named in your first week and
put the app in front of one of them, then write down what is enough
for them, not for you. Ask your agent to help you separate the two:

> Here is who this app is for and what I hoped it would do for them.
> Here is everything still on my list. Sort the list into what this
> person needs from this version, and what can wait for a later one,
> and tell me why for each.

Write the "later" list down where you will find it again, and publish
the version that is enough for now.

**From my apps.** The paragraph at the top of every app's `AGENTS.md`
is the bar I keep coming back to: "The goal is never a slick product.
It is a tool that makes someone more human" (stage 00,
[`00-why-we-build.md`](../../path/00-why-we-build.md)). A version can
meet that bar long before it is complete.

**In any tool.** The same in all three, because enough is a decision,
not a build.

**Go deeper.** Read what principle 15 means in full in
[PRINCIPLES.md](../../human-shaped/PRINCIPLES.md), and then principle
10, which counts what you learned as success even when the app is not
finished.

**Who to ask.** One of the people you named, first, and your teacher
second, because this is a decision no agent can make for you.

Enough for one named person, for now.
