# Stuck publishing

*Written by Claude, awaiting Ben's review.*

Publishing is releasing what you made into the world and letting others
play with it, so that it has a real audience, and it is the move my own
record shows I put off the longest. Tidbits Trivia reached someone
else's device two days after its first commit, and Archive Watch took
seven weeks ([research note 01](../../research/curriculum/01-mining-bens-process.md#9-someone-elses-device)),
and the second one was not better for the wait. These pages are here to
help you publish sooner than I did.

- [I am not ready to show anyone](not-ready.md)
- [I cannot get it onto someone else's device](someone-elses-device.md)
- [I do not know when it is enough](when-is-it-enough.md)
- [Life got in the way this week](life.md)

If none of these is your page, go back to [the ladder](../README.md#the-ladder-for-any-kind-of-stuck).
