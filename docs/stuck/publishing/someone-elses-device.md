# I cannot get it onto someone else's device

*Written by Claude, awaiting Ben's review.*

The app runs for you, and the person you most want to try it cannot get
it: they have a different phone, the store wants an account you do not
have, or the build only exists on your computer. Stores are slow and
some of them cost money, and none of that should stand between your app
and the first person it is for. Stage 05 has a whole section on the free
ways in, written for exactly this
([`05-shipping.md`](../../path/05-shipping.md#sharing-it-for-free)).

**Ask yourself first.** What is the simplest place this person could
open my app today, even if it is not the place it will finally live?

**Try this.** Start with the web, which reaches almost every device for
free, and ask your agent to get you there:

> I want someone with a different phone to try my app today, for free.
> Tell me the simplest way from what is in the template, starting with
> the web version, and what I need to do myself (a sign-in or a button)
> versus what you can do.

Do the human steps it names yourself, then send the person the address
and watch, if you can, while they open it for the first time.

**From my apps.** Three of my four apps started on the web for exactly
this reason: "I'm testing on the web, which is the easiest way for me to
see the progress" (prompt archive, Tidbits Trivia, June 17, 2026), and
BOBA Playbook's web search shipped the day the project began
(BOBA-Playbook c7c5b6f, April 3, 2026), both read in
[research note 01](../../research/curriculum/01-mining-bens-process.md#4-one-platform-i-could-see-easily).

**In each tool.** The same in Claude Code and Antigravity. On the open
path with 8 or 16 GB of memory, the installable web app is the
recommended second platform, rather than a native app that needs more
building power ([research note 04](../../research/curriculum/04-open-pathway.md)).

**Go deeper.** Read stage 05's comparison of the free and paid ways
onto Android and iPhone, with what each costs and what it limits.

**Who to ask.** Your teacher, for store accounts and signing, which are
the steps where it helps most to have someone who has been through the
same screens.

The web first, today, for one person.
