# Ear Drama for schools

Students read radio plays aloud together and record them as "ear dramas."
Ear Drama is free, has no ads, and is built so a class can do this without
student accounts, emails, or stored voices.

## For teachers: the one-minute version

- **No student accounts.** You type first names (or nicknames, or "Group 3").
  Students join with a class code and tap their name.
- **No student emails, birth dates, or photos.** We never ask.
- **Voices pass through.** A student's recording travels through our server
  to your device and is not kept on our server.
- **You decide.** You cast, review (Approve or Again), and keep or delete the
  recordings.
- **Under 13:** turn on "Under 13" when you make the class. Students can read
  together right away. Before their takes can be sent to you, you confirm
  that your school allows it. Under-13 work is **never published**.
- **13 and over:** you may publish a finished play to the Internet Archive
  under **your** name. Students' names are not published.
- **Check first:** if your school has a list of approved apps, ask for Ear
  Drama to be reviewed. Send your reviewer this page.

### Our commitments

These follow the Student Privacy Pledge and the NDPA's standard terms:

1. **School purposes only.** We use student information only to provide Ear
   Drama to the school.
2. **No selling.** We never sell or rent student information.
3. **No ads.** We never show ads or use student information to target ads.
4. **No profiles.** We never build a profile of a student except to run their
   class.
5. **The school is in charge.** Student information is under the school's
   control. We act as a "school official" with a legitimate educational
   interest, under the school's direct control, as FERPA allows. We use it
   only as the school directs.
6. **Minimum data.** We collect only what a class needs, listed above.
7. **Delete on request and at the end.** A teacher can delete a class at any
   time [BUILD]. A school can ask us to delete its data and we will within [30]
   days, and confirm in writing. [Classes end on [July 31] each year unless the
   teacher extends them, and their data is deleted then. BUILD]
8. **Access and correction.** Parents and eligible students request access or
   correction through the school, as FERPA provides. The teacher can rename or
   remove a student in the app. If a parent writes to us, we refer them to the
   school and help the school answer within [30] days.
9. **Security.** We keep a written security program aligned with the NIST
   Cybersecurity Framework, review it yearly, encrypt data in transit, and
   store keys only as hashes. [Link to summary.]
10. **Breach notice.** If student information is accessed without permission,
    we tell the school within [72 hours] of confirming it, with what happened,
    what data, and what we are doing, so the school can meet its own duties.
11. **No surprise changes.** We will not change how we use student information
    without telling schools first and getting their agreement; a change never
    applies to data we already hold.
12. **If Ear Drama ends or is sold,** student information is deleted, or kept
    under these same promises, and schools are told first.
13. **No AI training.** We never use student recordings or information to
    train AI.

### Children under 13 (COPPA)

COPPA lets a school give consent for an app used only for school purposes, in
place of each parent, following the FTC's guidance. In Ear Drama:

- The teacher marks a class "Under 13" when making it; it cannot be changed
  later.
- Until the teacher confirms the school's authorization, students can read
  together, but no student's voice leaves their device.
- The decision belongs to the school or district, not to a teacher alone (as
  the FTC's guidance says). The teacher confirms, in the app, that their
  school or district has approved Ear Drama for the class [owner: the
  confirmation words], and names the school or district. If the district has
  signed our data privacy agreement, that is the school's authorization on
  record.
- A school can confirm or withdraw its authorization at any time by writing
  to us. Withdrawing deletes the class's information [BUILD].
- Under-13 work is never published. The teacher can export a recording file to
  share with families the school's own way.
- On request, we give the school a description of what we collect and how we
  use it, so it can tell parents. A parent may ask the school to review or
  delete their child's information, or to stop its collection.
