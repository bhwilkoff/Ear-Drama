BACKGROUND AUDIO (Guideline 2.5.4): Ear Drama is an audio player. It plays old-time radio episodes and live radio "stations" (a real broadcast evening played on its own clock), and it plays ear dramas people record in the app. Listening continues when the person leaves the app: press the TV button during an episode and the audio keeps playing, with Ear Drama in Now Playing in Control Center, where it can be paused, resumed, or skipped. This is why the app declares the "audio" background mode. A screen recording on a physical Apple TV 4K is attached in App Review Information: it starts an episode, goes to the Home Screen, and shows Ear Drama still playing in Now Playing with the time advancing.

HOW TO SEE IT: open Ear Drama, choose Listen Live on Home (or any show, an episode, Play), then press the TV button. Open Control Center to see Ear Drama in Now Playing.

1. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Ear Drama is a free, ad-free place to hear drama made for the ear: old-time radio and radio drama from the Internet Archive, and new ear dramas that people record from play scripts in the app on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. On Apple TV it is for listening: series and episodes with dates and casts, live stations, and a read-along panel that shows the script as a recorded ear drama plays.

2. HOW TO USE THE MAIN FEATURES (no login needed)
- Home: "On the air" plays a station right away (Listen Live).
- Browse and Search: find any series or episode; select an episode and Play.
- Library: what you have played and queued.
Signing in (Sign in with Apple, optional) only syncs listening between devices.

3. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Internet Archive (archive.org): the audio streams and the recordings' public metadata.
- eardrama.org (GitHub Pages): our published catalog data.
- Our own server (a Cloudflare Worker): optional accounts and sync.
- Sign in with Apple: authentication. No analytics, no ads, no payments, no AI services.

4. REGIONS
The app works the same in every region.

5. THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Ear Drama does not host audio; it streams recordings that the Internet Archive publishes publicly, each linked to its archive.org source. Recordings are removed on request: https://eardrama.org/takedown/ (answered within 48 hours), and Learning is Change, Inc. has a designated DMCA agent registered with the U.S. Copyright Office (DMCA-1083942).
