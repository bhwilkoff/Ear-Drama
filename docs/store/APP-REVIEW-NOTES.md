1. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Ear Drama is a free, ad-free place to listen to old-time radio and radio drama from the Internet Archive, and to read and record radio plays yourself. It is for anyone who enjoys audio drama: listeners who want an evening of classic radio, and students, teachers, families and amateur actors who want to perform a play together. It solves a real problem: the Internet Archive holds tens of thousands of these broadcasts, but as raw uploads they are hard to find, date and listen to. Ear Drama organizes them into series and episodes with dates and casts, rebuilds real broadcast evenings as live "stations", and pairs listening with a script reader and a recording studio so people can make new radio plays from public-domain scripts.

2. HOW TO USE THE MAIN FEATURES (no login needed)
- Home: "On the air" plays a station right away (Listen Live); the Listen, Find a show and Record a play buttons lead to each part of the app.
- Browse and Search: find any series or episode; tap an episode and Play.
- Record tab: choose a play (Today's play, or the Plays list), tap Read to open the script reader, choose "I'm playing" and a part, then Record. Each of your lines is recorded take by take; the other parts are read by on-device voices. Recordings stay on the device.
- Library: what you have played, queued and downloaded.
Signing in (Sign in with Apple, optional) only syncs listening across devices and enables shared productions. To review sign-in, use your own Apple ID; Account in Library has Sign Out and Delete Account (deletes the account and its data on our server).
User content: a person can invite others to a shared production by link. Each shared production has Report and Block for every member (People section), and a director can remove any line.

3. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Internet Archive (archive.org): the audio streams and the recordings' public metadata.
- Project Gutenberg: public-domain play texts (pre-processed into our catalog).
- eardrama.org (GitHub Pages): our published catalog data and pages.
- Our own server (a Cloudflare Worker with a database): optional accounts, sync, shared productions, reports and blocks.
- Sign in with Apple (and Sign in with Google on the web and Android): authentication.
- Apple's on-device speech synthesis (AVSpeechSynthesizer): the stand-in voices. Nothing is sent to an AI service; no analytics, no ads, no payments.

4. REGIONS
The app works the same in every region; there are no regional differences in features or content.

5. THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Ear Drama does not host audio; it streams recordings that the Internet Archive publishes publicly, with each recording linked to its archive.org source. Recordings are removed on request: a take-down process is at https://eardrama.org/takedown/ (complete notices are answered within 48 hours), and Learning is Change, Inc. has a designated DMCA agent registered with the U.S. Copyright Office (DMCA-1083942). Play scripts offered for reading and recording are limited to texts whose public-domain status in the U.S. is documented on their Project Gutenberg record (published before 1931, or marked public domain by Project Gutenberg); each play carries that evidence.
