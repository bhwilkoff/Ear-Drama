# A request to the Generic Radio Workshop (draft for the owner to send)

Decision 067: ask before any Generic Radio Workshop transcription is used. The
owner sends this from their own address; edit freely. Nothing ships from GRW's
text until they answer yes.

**Subject:** Permission to use GRW script transcriptions for amateur radio-play recordings

Hello,

I run Ear Drama (eardrama.org), a free, ad-free app for listening to old-time
radio from the Internet Archive. It has a feature where families, classes, and
friends record a radio play together, one part at a time, from a script.

Your transcriptions are the best source of radio scripts anywhere, and our
reader can follow about 950 of them. We would only use scripts whose broadcast
is in the public domain or whose rights we can document separately, and we
would credit the Generic Radio Workshop and the transcriber on every one.

May we use your transcriptions this way? If you would rather we did not, or
want conditions (a link back, a list you approve, anything else), we will
follow them. We will not use any of your transcriptions without your
permission.

Thank you for the years of work behind the Workshop.

Ben Wilkoff
Ear Drama, eardrama.org
