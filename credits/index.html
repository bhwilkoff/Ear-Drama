Credits

Internet Archive
Every recording plays from archive.org, where collectors uploaded it.
Old Time Radio Researchers (OTRR)
Episode logs that name the file playing each broadcast, with its credits; the series' alternate titles; and the episode sets on archive.org.
OTRRpedia
Records of programs and the people who made them.
RadioEchoes
Genres for each series, and a second record of which episodes survive.
RadioGOLDINdex
J. David Goldin's database of radio programs, held by the University of Missouri–Kansas City Libraries: air dates and credits.
The Generic Radio Workshop
Radio scripts, read for their credits as written.
LibriVox
Volunteer recordings of public-domain texts, including full-cast plays.
Library of Congress Prints and Photographs
Photographs of radio performers with no known restrictions.
Smithsonian Open Access
Portraits released under CC0, most from the National Portrait Gallery.
Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons
Dates and identifiers for people, and portraits, each shown with its own license.
