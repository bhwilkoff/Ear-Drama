Take-down requests

Ear Drama plays recordings hosted by the Internet Archive. When no rights claim is known for a recording, it is kept and labeled "No rights claim known. Removed on request." Ear Drama does not call these recordings public domain.

What to send

  1. The link to each episode or series on eardrama.org.
  2. The work you hold rights in, and how you hold them.
  3. Your name, postal address, phone number, and email address.
  4. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorized by the rights holder, its agent, or the law.
  5. A statement that the information in the request is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the rights holder or authorized to act for them.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.

These are the elements of a notice under 17 U.S.C. §512(c)(3).

What happens next

A complete notice is answered within one week. The recording comes off the next time the catalog is built, in every app and on this site, and stays off. Your details are kept private and erased three years after the case closes (Privacy).
