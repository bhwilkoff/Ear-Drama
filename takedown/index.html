Take-down requests

Ear Drama plays recordings hosted by the Internet Archive. When no rights claim is known for a recording, it is kept and labeled "No rights claim known. Removed on request." Ear Drama does not call these recordings public domain.

What to send

  1. The link to each episode or series on eardrama.org.
  2. The work you hold rights in, and how you hold them.
  3. The claim in a form a reader can open: a registration or renewal record, a schedule filed with the U.S. Copyright Office under 17 U.S.C. §1401(f)(5), or another published record that names the claimant.
  4. Your name, postal address, phone number, and email address.
  5. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorized by the rights holder, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement that the information in the request is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the rights holder or authorized to act for them.
  7. Your physical or electronic signature.

Items 4 through 7 are the elements of a notice under 17 U.S.C. §512(c)(3).

Where to send it

By email to the address on this page.

What happens next

A recording is removed once its claim can be opened and read. The removal takes effect the next time the catalog is built.
